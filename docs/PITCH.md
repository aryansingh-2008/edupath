# EduPath - Pitch Deck & Executive Summary

> **Tagline**: *"Your skills today. Your career tomorrow. One adaptive path."*

---

## 1. The Core Problem
- **The Information Paradox**: The internet has infinite learning resources, yet developer career transitions have an 85% abandonment rate.
- **The Blind Spot**: Students don't know what they know, what they lack, or what to build to prove it to employers.
- **The Static Trap**: Existing platforms (Coursera, Udemy, YouTube) serve rigid, linear playlists. When a learner hits a wall on day 14, the curriculum marches forward blind, causing confusion, frustration, and dropout.

---

## 2. The Solution: EduPath Career Operating System
EduPath replaces static playlists with an **Autonomous Career Navigation Agent**:
1. **Intelligent Ingestion**: Parses resumes, project repos, and portfolios to build a confidence-scored skill profile backed by evidence.
2. **Precision Gap Analysis**: Maps capabilities against real-time industry role requirements to highlight high-priority bottlenecks.
3. **Adaptive Replanning Engine**: Continuously observes practice performance, detects recurring struggles, and recalibrates the learning path in real time with complete explainability.
4. **Evidence-Building Projects**: Transitions skills from "Claimed" to "Demonstrated" through generated capstones.

---

## 3. The 9-Agent Multi-Agent Swarm
- `Profile Agent`: Parses unstructured resume text into verifiable capabilities.
- `Skill Intelligence Agent`: Normalizes terms to canonical ontologies.
- `Gap Agent`: Computes prioritized deltas against target benchmarks.
- `Roadmap Planner Agent`: Synthesizes dependency DAGs into weekly milestones.
- `Resource Agent`: Ranks curated, verified learning materials.
- `Practice Agent`: Calibrates diagnostic questions to the learner's zone of proximal development.
- `Progress Agent`: Updates proficiency scores based on empirical practice accuracy.
- `Adaptive Planner Agent`: Detects bottlenecks and orchestrates route mutations.
- `Mentor Agent`: Provides real-time contextual coaching with full awareness of learner state.

---
