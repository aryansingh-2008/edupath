# EduPath - 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

This script is engineered for a high-impact, 3-minute live presentation to hackathon judges. It follows a tight, narrative-driven arc centered around the primary demo persona: **Alex**, an aspiring Full Stack Developer.

---

## Pitch Narrative Overview

- **Target Time**: 3 minutes (180 seconds)
- **Lead Character**: Alex (Junior developer, knows JS/React, wants to land a Full Stack role)
- **The Core Conflict**: Alex keeps failing interviews because static roadmaps don't know what he is missing or when he gets stuck.
- **The WOW Moment**: Alex struggles on SQL practice $\to$ EduPath detects the bottleneck in real time $\to$ The roadmap visibly adapts with an explainable reason.

---

## Timed Scene-by-Scene Script

### Scene 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:20) [20s]
*Visual: Slide or EduPath Landing Page Hero with dynamic animated trajectory.*
- **Presenter**: 
  > "Every developer has experienced this: You want to become a Full Stack Engineer or AI Architect. You open YouTube, Coursera, and GitHub, and you're hit with 500 hours of content. You don't know what you actually know, what you're missing, or what to build. 
  > 
  > Existing platforms give everyone the exact same rigid 12-week course. Today, we're introducing **EduPath**: not a course platform, but an autonomous career navigation system that adapts your path as you learn."

---

### Scene 2: Meet Alex & Profile Extraction (0:20 – 0:50) [30s]
*Action: Click "Launch Demo Mode" &rarr; Show Alex's Profile.*
- **Presenter**: 
  > "Meet Alex. Alex is applying for Full Stack roles. Alex uploaded his resume. 
  > 
  > Notice what our **Profile Agent** did: It didn't just dump a list of keywords. It extracted skills with **explicit evidence**. JavaScript at 78% supported by 2 production projects. React at 62%. But look down: SQL is at 34%, and System Design is at 18%. 
  > 
  > Every skill has an evidence trail, distinguishing between claimed skills and demonstrated capability."

---

### Scene 3: Skill Constellation & Prioritized Gaps (0:50 – 1:20) [30s]
*Action: Switch to "Skills" Tab & click SQL node.*
- **Presenter**: 
  > "On our **Skill Map**, EduPath overlays Alex's current capabilities against canonical industry requirements. 
  > 
  > Our **Gap Agent** immediately flags SQL as a **Critical Bottleneck**—not just because it's low, but because 8 out of 10 target competencies depend on it. 
  > 
  > Clicking 'Why This?' shows the AI's exact rationale: 'Required for backend persistence; prerequisite for database indexing and authentication.'"

---

### Scene 4: The Dynamic Roadmap & Today's Mission (1:20 – 1:50) [30s]
*Action: Switch to "Path" Tab, then "Today's Mission".*
- **Presenter**: 
  > "Our **Roadmap Agent** synthesizes a prioritized sequence of milestones. Week 1 is SQL Foundations; Week 2 is Query Mastery; followed by Backend APIs and System Design.
  > 
  > Alex's dashboard doesn't overwhelm him with 40 tabs. It gives him **Today's Mission**: 'Master SQL JOINs — 25 minutes.' Let's start the mission."

---

### Scene 5: The Climax — Struggle & Real-Time Adaptation (1:50 – 2:30) [40s]
*Action: Switch to "Practice" Tab & click "Simulate SQL Struggle" in the Demo Control Panel.*
- **Presenter**: 
  > "Alex enters the **Practice Lab**. He takes a diagnostic quiz on SQL queries. 
  > 
  > Watch what happens: Alex fails two consecutive INNER and LEFT JOIN questions. 
  > 
  > On a static website, Alex would be stuck or forced to re-watch a 2-hour video. 
  > 
  > But look at the top of EduPath: **Learning Bottleneck Detected**. 
  > 
  > Our **Adaptive Planner Agent** immediately stepped in. Look at this explainability card:
  > - **Trigger**: Repeated difficulty with JOIN concepts (2 of 3 failed).
  > - **Decision**: SQL foundation requires reinforcement.
  > - **Action**: Inserted 'SQL JOIN Recovery Module' & shifted backend module by 2 days.
  > 
  > Look at the roadmap timeline: It dynamically re-ordered itself in real time!"

---

### Scene 6: Capstone Project & Career Readiness (2:30 – 2:50) [20s]
*Action: Switch to "Projects" Tab & "Career Readiness".*
- **Presenter**: 
  > "Once Alex completes the recovery practice, EduPath's **Project Builder** generates a hands-on project: 'Build a Multi-Tenant Expense API' with PostgreSQL. 
  > 
  > Completing this project converts Alex's SQL skill from 'Claimed' to 'Demonstrated' in his career readiness radar. His full-stack readiness score jumps from 42% to 68%."

---

### Scene 7: Conclusion & Value Proposition (2:50 – 3:00) [10s]
*Visual: Career Readiness Radar & Agent Activity Panel.*
- **Presenter**: 
  > "EduPath doesn't give everyone the same roadmap. It builds the roadmap around you, watches how you perform, and adapts when you struggle. 
  > 
  > Your skills today. Your career tomorrow. One adaptive path. Thank you!"
